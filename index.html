PokerHQ — User Guide

What PokerHQ does

PokerHQ is a private command center for live tournament poker. It tracks your bankroll, your schedule, your sessions, your key hands, and the lessons you take from them — and ties them together so each run makes the next one sharper. This guide walks the whole app end to end:

  • Signing in and installing PokerHQ as a desktop or phone app
  • Setting your bankroll rule so every buy-in is auto-graded against it
  • Planning a slate from the calendar, pinning the events you'll actually play, and chasing seats through satellites
  • Running a live session with the timer, hand capture (typed or dictated by voice), and mental check-ins
  • Logging the result, reviewing it with debriefs and heatmaps, and turning it into a study note
  • Letting AI research current poker news and notable events for you — with verifiable sources
  • Reading your dashboard insights — bankroll curve, variance & downswing, hourly rate, monthly goals, and your next planned event
  • Tracking wallet, bankroll, and staking money through the Treasury

Your data lives in your account and syncs across every device automatically — desktop, laptop, and phone all show the same numbers, online or offline.

♠
The nav groups Across the top (and along the bottom on a phone) are HOME, PLAN, PLAY, TREASURY, REVIEW, IMPROVE, and ❓ HELP (this guide). HOME is your dashboard; PLAN through IMPROVE each open a row of sub-tabs. This guide follows them in the order you'd use them for a real event.

The PokerHQ loop

The app is built around a four-phase cycle, with a one-time Setup feeding into it. Plan what to play, play it well, review what happened, and improve before the next one — then around again.

The loop — setup feeds a four-phase cycle

1  Sign in & install

The first time you open PokerHQ you'll see a login card. Everything behind it is private to your Google account.

  1. 1
    Open the app and the PokerHQ login card appears.
  2. 2
    Click Sign in with Google and choose your account. The app loads with your data synced.
  3. 3
    Install it (optional but recommended): click ⤓ INSTALL APP in the top bar — or Install as desktop app on the login card. It becomes a standalone window with the spade icon, pinnable to your taskbar or dock.
📲
Installing on iPhone On iOS, open the app in Safari, tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen. It then launches full-screen like a native app and works offline at the casino.
⚠️
Sign in with the same account on every device Your bankroll, sessions, and hands are tied to your Google account. Use a different account and you'll see an empty app with an "access denied" message — sign out and back in with the right one to restore your data.

2  Set your bankroll

This is the one number that powers the whole app. Every buy-in on your calendar is auto-graded against it, so set it before you plan anything.

  1. 1
    Go to PLAY → ♦ Log Session and find the Bankroll Setup card at the top.
  2. 2
    Pick a BRM Rule — Conservative (20 buy-ins), Standard (15), or Aggressive (10). This sets how many buy-ins your roll should cover.
  3. 3
    Your bankroll figure itself is read-only here — it moves automatically as you log sessions and make Treasury transfers. To set a starting balance, use a Treasury Deposit (see Treasury).
How the rule grades a buy-in — worked example at ₱45,000
💡
Read it on the dashboard HOME shows your current bankroll, the recommended and stretch buy-ins, and how many shots you have at your recommended stake — plus your live bankroll-over-time chart, a variance & downswing read, your hourly rate, this month's goals, and your next planned event. See Dashboard insights for the full tour.

3  Plan the slate

PLAN has two sub-tabs: 📅 Calendar for events and 🛰 Satellites for chasing seats you can't afford directly.

3.1 — Add an event to the calendar

  1. 1
    Go to PLAN → 📅 Calendar and click + ADD TOURNAMENT.
  2. 2
    Fill in Date, Tournament Name, Venue, Buy-in, GTD, and Structure. This example: Solaire Sunday Main · ₱5,500 · ₱2,000,000 GTD.
  3. 3
    Save. The event appears in the month grid and the list view, auto-tagged against your bankroll — here, SKIP, because ₱5,500 is over the ₱5,000 stretch.
🗂
Long festival slates stay tidy In the list view, events are grouped by series. Tap a series header to collapse or expand that group, or use ⊕ EXPAND ALL / ⊖ COLLAPSE ALL. Multi-event series start collapsed so a packed festival opens as a compact list of headers rather than a wall of rows; your collapse choices are remembered.

3.2 — Mark the events you're playing

Grading tells you what you can afford; pinning records what you've actually decided to play. The two are independent — you might pin a STRETCH event you're committed to, or skip a TARGET you're not traveling for.

  1. 1
    In the Calendar list view, tap the ☆ on any event row to pin it (it fills to ★). Tap again to unpin.
  2. 2
    Pinned events collect in the ★ Playing These card at the top of the calendar, soonest first, with a running buy-in commitment — e.g. "3 events · ₱50,000 committed" — so you can see your total spend at a glance.
  3. 3
    Use the ★ PLANNED ONLY toggle (next to MONTH / LIST) to filter both the month grid and the list down to just your pinned events. Toggle it off to see the full slate again.
★
Your plan surfaces on HOME The soonest event you've pinned shows in a "Planning to play" block on the dashboard — above the day's auto-surfaced slate — so the next thing you've committed to is the first thing you see. Tap it to jump straight to its calendar row.

3.3 — Turn a SKIP into a seat with satellites

The Main is graded SKIP at ₱5,500 — but a ₱1,100 satellite is well within your recommended ₱3,000. Track that path under Satellites.

  1. 1
    Go to PLAN → 🛰 Satellites and set the target: the event you're chasing and its direct buy-in — Solaire Sunday Main · ₱5,500.
  2. 2
    Each time you play a satellite, log it: date, name, buy-in, and result — Seat Won, Did Not Win, or Pending. This example: a ₱1,100 satellite, Seat Won.
  3. 3
    The hero stats show satellites played, seats won, total invested, and money saved versus buying in directly — here, one ₱1,100 entry bought a ₱5,500 seat, so ₱4,400 saved.
▶
Start straight from the calendar When an event is live, open the Calendar list view and click ▶ START SESSION on its row. PokerHQ opens an active session pre-filled with that tournament's name, venue, and buy-in — no re-typing.

3.4 — Get reminders before events

PokerHQ has two ways to make sure an event doesn't slip past you — use either or both.

  1. 1
    In-app email reminders. On PLAN → 📅 Calendar, find the Email Reminders card, tick Email me before target events, and set how many days ahead. PokerHQ then emails your account address before each upcoming TARGET and STRETCH event — once each, even while the app is closed.
  2. 2
    Calendar file (.ics). ⤓ EXPORT .ICS downloads those same upcoming target and stretch events (SKIP events left out), each with a one-day-ahead reminder built in. Import it into Google or Apple Calendar once and your own calendar handles the rest.

4  Start a session

An active session holds your live context — the timer, your bullet count, your mental state, and quick-capture buttons — so everything you log mid-event stays attached to the result.

A live session — what stays attached
  1. 1
    Start the session — either ▶ START SESSION from the calendar, or MANUAL SESSION on the dashboard for an unplanned sit-down.
  2. 2
    Run the timer from the dashboard widget — START when you sit, STOP on break, and LOG ↗ to drop the elapsed hours straight into the session.
  3. 3
    Set your readiness with one tap — ROUGH / STEADY / SHARP fills focus, energy, sleep, and food in one move, or rate each 1–10 yourself.
  4. 4
    Track re-entries with the bullet counter each time you fire again.
🧠
Before you sit — your lessons resurface The active-session surface shows a "Before you sit" prep block: your last few hand lessons and most recent study note, so the things you meant to fix are in front of you at the one moment they matter. If you've tagged opponents at this venue, the venue scouting panel lists them right there too.

5  Capture hands

The best time to record a hand is while it's fresh. Capture it during the session and it links to this run automatically.

5.1 — Log a hand by hand

  1. 1
    From the active session, click CAPTURE HAND (or go to PLAY → 🃏 Hands → + LOG HAND).
  2. 2
    Set the result (Won / Lost / Fold), a short title, the full description, and the lesson you took from it.
  3. 3
    Optionally fill the replay fields — hero & villain positions, stacks/blinds, board runout, and the action street by street. These power the REPLAY view later.

5.2 — Or dictate it (voice to hand)

On the Hands tab, 🎙 VOICE TO HAND turns a spoken recap into a structured hand — record it right in the app, no separate voice-memo app needed.

  1. 1
    Tap 🎤 RECORD, allow the microphone, and talk through the hand naturally. Tap ⏹ STOP & TRANSCRIBE — Whisper transcribes your audio straight into the box.
  2. 2
    Click ✨ STRUCTURE WITH AI — Claude turns the transcript into result, title, description, and lesson.
  3. 3
    Review, edit anything, and SAVE HAND.
🔑
This needs two API keys, set once per device Recording uses an OpenAI key (Whisper); structuring uses an Anthropic key (Claude). Add both in IMPROVE → ♥ Strategy → AI Assistant — they're stored on that device only, never synced. See AI features & keys.

6  Log the result

When the tournament ends, fill in the result and PokerHQ does the rest — bankroll, stats, and the chart all update.

  1. 1
    On PLAY → ♦ Log Session, complete the result fields: Buy-in, Rebuys/Add-ons, Field Size, Finish Position, Prize, and Hours.
  2. 2
    If your action was sold, fill the staking fields — Package, Player Share %, Markup, and Named Backers. Optional, and it doesn't change your core log.
  3. 3
    Click LOG SESSION ↗. This example: 8th of 142 for ₱18,000 off a ₱5,500 entry.
✅
What happens on save Your bankroll moves by the session's profit/loss, the dashboard stats and bankroll chart refresh, any hands you captured link in, and a toast offers to jump straight to the session debrief. The mental ratings are what make the heatmaps meaningful later — worth the ten seconds.

7  Review the run

REVIEW has two sub-tabs: ♦ History for the full session log and 🔥 Heatmap for patterns across all of them.

7.1 — Find and open a session

  1. 1
    On REVIEW → ♦ History, use the filter bar — search by name/venue/notes, filter by result, or set a date range.
  2. 2
    Click any column header to sort — tap P&L to bring your biggest swings to the top, tap again to reverse, a third time to reset.
  3. 3
    Click a row to open the session detail: the key stats, staking breakdown, linked hands, and the debrief.

7.2 — Run the debrief

Inside a session, Session Debrief reads your result, mental ratings, linked hands, and history at that venue, then writes a short, concrete "what went well / what hurt / what to review next / next-session focus." This part is free and needs no key. With an Anthropic key set in AI Assistant, ENHANCE WITH AI layers a deeper Claude pass on top.

7.3 — Read the heatmaps

🔥 Heatmap turns your whole history into patterns: P&L by venue, by buy-in range, ITM rate, ROI by event, a 12-month calendar, and — uniquely — results versus your mental state. That last one answers questions like "do I actually run worse on low sleep?" with your own data.

8  Improve

IMPROVE closes the loop: ♥ Strategy for notes and weekly briefings, and 👤 Opponents for villain reads.

8.1 — Capture a lesson

  1. 1
    On IMPROVE → ♥ Strategy, add a manual note — give it a week, a topic, and the insight.
  2. 2
    Or paste a weekly intelligence briefing (JSON) into the import box. PokerHQ splits it into calendar events, news, strategy insights, and a spotlight — deduplicating as it goes.

8.2 — Let AI research the poker world

On IMPROVE → ♥ Strategy, the 🔎 AI Poker Research card searches the live web for what's worth knowing and files it into your feeds. Pick a Focus, run it, and each result is badged ✓ Verified source or ⚠ Unverified. + Save an item — or Save All Verified — to drop it into your news feed or strategy notes with a clickable source link.

🛡️
Why the "verified" badge matters PokerHQ cross-checks every item's link against the pages Claude actually searched — anything it can't corroborate is marked ⚠ Unverified and left out of Save All Verified. A "verified" badge means the page was really fetched, not that the summary is flawless, so click through before relying on anything important. Needs your Anthropic key; web search adds a small per-search cost.

8.3 — Keep opponent reads

  1. 1
    On 👤 Opponents, click + ADD VILLAIN — name, venue, tags (loose, aggressive, calling station…), and a tendency note.
  2. 2
    When you next start a session at that venue, your tagged players surface automatically in the active-session venue scouting panel.

Treasury & staking

TREASURY → 💼 separates your wallet (all your poker money) from your bankroll (the part in play), and records every movement in a typed ledger.

🤝
Backer settlement reports If you sell action, the staking fields on each session feed a backer package report you can export as a PDF — per-backer cost, share, and net for a whole festival package. Find it under the staking summary on the Log Session tab.

Editing & undo

Nothing in PokerHQ is permanent on a single tap.

  • Edit anything — sessions, hands, tournaments, opponents, and satellites all have a ✎ button. Editing a session re-opens the full form with every field pre-filled, and correcting a prize or buy-in adjusts your bankroll by the difference automatically.
  • Undo a delete — deleting anything shows an ↩ UNDO toast for six seconds. Tap it and the record comes back exactly where it was; for sessions, the bankroll change reverses too.
  • Clear in bulk — ↺ CLEAR ALL SESSIONS wipes every session and reverses their combined effect on your bankroll, and ↺ RESET WALLET zeroes the wallet and its ledger. Both ask first and both show an ↩ UNDO toast.

Common questions

How do I mark which events I'm actually going to play?

In PLAN → Calendar, switch to the list view and tap the ☆ on any event to pin it (★). Pinned events gather in the ★ Playing These card at the top of the calendar with a running buy-in total, and the soonest one shows in a "Planning to play" block on HOME. Use the ★ PLANNED ONLY toggle to filter the whole calendar to just your picks.

What's the difference between a TARGET grade and pinning an event?

They answer different questions. The grade (TARGET / STRETCH / SKIP) is automatic and answers "can I afford this under my bankroll rule?" Pinning (★) is your own choice and answers "have I decided to play it?" You can pin a STRETCH event you're committed to, or leave a TARGET unpinned if you're not traveling for it. Pinning never changes the grade.

I pinned an event but it disappeared from the shortlist.

The ★ Playing These card and the HOME "Planning to play" block only show upcoming pinned events — once an event's date has passed it drops off automatically, even though it stays pinned in the full list. The card is meant to show what's still ahead of you.

How do I get updates — do I need to refresh?

PokerHQ updates itself. When a new version is deployed, a gold "Update available · Reload" banner appears in the app — click Reload and you're on the latest. You can keep using the current version until you're ready; nothing is lost.

I signed in but the app is empty / says access denied.

You're signed in with a different Google account than the one your data is under. Click the sign-out button in the top bar and sign back in with the correct account. Your data is safe — it's tied to the right account, not lost.

Why is my target event graded SKIP?

Its buy-in is above your stretch limit (bankroll ÷ 9 on the Standard rule). That's the app telling you a direct buy-in is outside disciplined bankroll management. The move is a satellite — log the cheaper qualifier under PLAN → Satellites and chase the seat instead.

Do I need API keys to use PokerHQ?

No. Everything — planning, sessions, typed hand capture, review, heatmaps, treasury, the local session debrief — works without any key. Keys only power the optional AI features: an Anthropic key for hand-structuring and the AI-enhanced debrief, and an OpenAI key for the 🎤 voice recorder.

Can PokerHQ email me before a tournament?

Yes. On PLAN → Calendar, open the Email Reminders card, tick Email me before target events, and choose how many days ahead. PokerHQ emails your account address before each upcoming TARGET and STRETCH event — once each, even when the app is closed. Prefer your own calendar? ⤓ EXPORT .ICS imports the same events with reminders built in.

Will it work at the casino with no signal?

Yes. Once loaded (and especially once installed), PokerHQ runs offline. Anything you log while offline is saved on the device and syncs automatically the moment you're back online — the sync indicator in the top bar shows the current state.

What's the difference between my wallet and my bankroll?

The wallet is all your poker money; the bankroll is the slice currently in play that buy-in recommendations are based on. Move money between them with Treasury Top-up and Pullout transactions — for example, pull profit out to your wallet after a big cash to keep your in-play roll disciplined.
